Connect Budget to your bank

About 5 minutes, once. You'll create a BankSync account, start its free trial and connect your bank, then sign in from Budget. Budget never sees your bank login or your card.

Have ready: your phone with your bank's app (your bank sends a one-time code there), and a card for BankSync's free trial. CommBank may also ask for the last 4 digits of your NetBank ID.

1Create your BankSync account

Sign up with Google, or with your name, email and a password. BankSync emails you a link to confirm your address.

Create my BankSync account

2Connect your bank

On BankSync's home screen, tap Connect bank account (or Banks, then Connect bank), search for your bank and tap it.

3Start the free trial

Pick a plan. Starter covers one bank: US$5 a month, or US$48 a year. Your card is taken now and charged after 14 days; you can cancel any time in BankSync (Settings, Billing). You pay BankSync, not Budget.

4Approve at your bank

Your bank's own page opens. Enter your registered mobile number and the one-time code your bank sends to its app, then tick only the accounts you want to share: one is enough to start, and you can add more later.

5Back to Budget

Open Budget and tap Sign in with BankSync. Approve Budget, choose the accounts it may read, and your transactions arrive.

Questions

Is this safe?

It's Australia's open banking, the Consumer Data Right. You approve at your own bank, nobody else sees your bank login, and you can stop any time.

BankSync says the request expired

Its approval page lasts about 10 minutes. Close it, and in Budget tap Sign in with BankSync again.

Why do I need BankSync?

By law, an accredited company (or its representative) must hold your bank connection. BankSync does that, under Fiskil, which the ACCC has accredited and which keeps data in Australia. BankSync keeps no copy of your transactions.

Is Budget accredited?

It doesn't need to be. Accreditation is for companies that receive people's bank data, and we never do: your data goes from BankSync straight to Budget on your phone.

Why isn't the address a link in the app?

Google Play requires apps to sell things through Google's own payments. BankSync's plan is sold by BankSync, so an app on Play can't send you to its checkout with a tap; it can only tell you the address. It's a rule about payments, not about your safety or your data.

How do I stop?

Disconnect in Budget (Data sources), cancel the plan in BankSync (Settings, Billing), and withdraw the consent in BankSync (Settings, Data sharing) or in your bank's app.

How does Budget make money?

Right now it doesn't: Budget is free, with no ads, and your data is never sold.

Budget: privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026.

Budget is a budgeting app for your phone, made by XYZ Tech Labs in Australia. This page says what it does with your information. The short version: your money data stays on your phone, and nobody else gets it from us.

What the app reads

With your permission, Budget reads your bank accounts, their balances and your transactions. It only reads: it can never move money, pay anyone or change anything at your bank.

How it gets your bank data

Your bank connection is made and held by BankSync (Flagbase Pty Ltd), a representative of Fiskil Pty Ltd, an accredited data recipient under Australia's Consumer Data Right. You connect your bank on BankSync's pages and approve the sharing with your bank directly; your bank login is never given to Budget. BankSync's own terms and privacy policy cover that connection: banksync.io/terms.

If you connect PocketSmith instead, the app reads from your PocketSmith account with the key you give it, under PocketSmith's terms.

Where your data is kept

  • On your phone, in the app's private storage. The sign-in that lets the app read your data is kept in your phone's secure storage.
  • In backups, only if you make one; a backup goes where you choose to save it.
  • Nowhere else. Budget has no server: the developer never receives your bank data, your categories or anything else you put in the app.

What is shared

Nothing. Budget has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking that leaves your phone, and your information is never sold or passed on. The app talks only to the service that provides your bank data (BankSync or PocketSmith).

Stopping and deleting

  • Disconnect your bank in the app, or withdraw your consent in BankSync (Settings, Data sharing) or in your bank's app. Reading stops straight away.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it kept on your phone.
